Next gen health data

Good, quality data is the key. But you also need high response rates and systems that are intuitive for end users and administrators. It all starts with listening and communication, then we translate the conversation into the data system you need.

Problem

Health data collection tools haven't evolved at the rate of technology. More importantly, medical and health policy staff are asked to be technology experts. It is just a poor use of expertise and time.

Approach

Use templates and build on previous solutions to make implementation cost-effective, fast, and effective. Data coordinators understand the conventions of standardized forms. Administrators have access to data and manage users, questions, and responses.

Solution

Secure, easy to use systems which provide access to data at country, regional and global levels.
