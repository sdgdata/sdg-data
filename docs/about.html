HISTORY

We started at a time when Excel spreadsheets were the norm for collecting world-wide data. Our initial project successes quickly spread to other areas with the same business need: web-based, secure data collection with central and regional management of users and surveys. Just as important was access to response data in multiple formats, notably Stata, Excel, ODBC, and direct data editing of online data. This is what we do, and we do it well.
